# 🌐 Krynet Community Ports 🇮🇸

> **Independent community organization**
>
> Krynet Community Ports is an independent community organization providing **ports, alternative clients, integrations, experiments, compatibility layers, developer tooling, and other projects** related to the Krynet ecosystem.
>
> The organization is **not affiliated with, endorsed by, maintained by, reviewed by, partnered with, or used by Krynet, LLC or the Krynet Team.**

---

## ⚠️ About This Organization

Krynet Community Ports exists to give developers and users an independent place to build, maintain, experiment with, and preserve software related to the Krynet ecosystem.

The name **"Krynet"** is used only to identify the software and ecosystem that community projects interact with or are built around.

Projects hosted here are developed independently and may differ substantially from official Krynet software.

For official Krynet software, documentation, policies, UGC information, and announcements, use the official Krynet resources listed below.

## 🧩 Krynet UGC Program

> [!IMPORTANT]
> ### 🚫 Community Ports Is Not the Official UGC Program
>
> **Krynet Community is not part of Krynet, LLC's UGC Program.**
>
> The official Krynet UGC Program provides a way for creators to develop community content for Krynet, which may include:
>
> - 🧩 Plugins
> - 🎨 Themes
> - 🔌 Extensions
> - 🛠️ Other user-created content
> - 🛒 Store listings
>
> Depending on the official program's terms, eligible creators may be able to publish content for free or receive payment for qualifying content.
>
> Projects hosted by Krynet Community are **not automatically UGC Program projects**, even when they provide similar functionality.

### 🏪 Store & Creator Status

A community project should not be assumed to:

- 🛒 Be listed in the official Krynet Store
- 💰 Be eligible for official creator payments
- ✅ Have passed official Krynet review
- 🧩 Be approved UGC Program content
- 📜 Follow official UGC Program requirements

If an individual community project independently participates in an official Krynet program, that relationship should be explicitly confirmed through an official Krynet source.
